About the Doctor

A Decade of Dedicated
Oncology Care

Dr. Muhammod Mahmudur Rahman is a highly qualified and experienced oncologist with over 10 years of dedicated work in the field of cancer diagnosis, management, and treatment. He specializes in handling a wide range of oncology patients with compassion and clinical excellence.

Holding advanced degrees from Bangladesh Medical University, Dr. Rahman brings deep expertise in medical oncology - from standard chemotherapy protocols to cutting-edge immunotherapy and targeted therapy approaches.

He currently serves as Assistant Professor at Jamalpur Medical College Hospital and holds a regular chamber at Jamalpur Central Hospital Limited, providing expert oncology consultation to patients across the region.

Treatment Modalities

Specialized in Advanced
Cancer Treatment

Dr. Rahman offers a comprehensive range of evidence-based cancer treatment approaches tailored to each patient's specific condition.

01
Chemotherapy
Systemic drug-based cancer treatment delivered regularly at the hospital, targeting rapidly dividing cancer cells throughout the body.
02
Immunotherapy
Harnessing the body's own immune system to recognize, attack, and destroy cancer cells with improved precision and minimal side effects.
03
Targeted Therapy
Drugs designed to target specific molecular abnormalities in cancer cells, offering a more focused treatment with fewer side effects.
04
Radiotherapy
High-energy radiation used to shrink tumors and destroy cancer cells, often in combination with other treatment modalities.
05
Hormone Therapy
Treatment that blocks or lowers the level of hormones to slow or stop cancer growth - commonly used for breast and prostate cancers.
